About Me

BIM coordination, electrical design and construction documentation.

My Story

I’m an Electrical BIM Engineer with experience in BIM coordination, electrical design and construction documentation for residential and mixed-use projects. I work with Autodesk Revit, Navisworks Manage, Dynamo, Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC) and AutoCAD across power, lighting and low-current systems, covering clash detection, shop drawings, BOQs, panel schedules, SLDs, voltage drop calculations and multidisciplinary coordination. I’m familiar with ISO 19650, BIM deliverables and QA/QC procedures. I hold a B.Sc. in Electrical Power and Machines Engineering from Damietta University.
